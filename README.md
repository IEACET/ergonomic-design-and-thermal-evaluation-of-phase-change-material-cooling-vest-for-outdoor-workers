# Ergonomic design and thermal evaluation of phase change material cooling vest for outdoor workers 

## 📝 Description
Outdoor and industrial workers such as traffic police, delivery riders, and construction 
personnel are frequently exposed to high temperatures and intense sunlight for prolonged 
durations. Continuous exposure to such environments results in heat stress, fatigue, 
dehydration, and reduced work efficiency, posing significant risks to occupational health and 
safety. In this context, wearable cooling technologies using Phase Change Materials (PCMs) 
have emerged as an effective and sustainable solution for maintaining thermal comfort.
